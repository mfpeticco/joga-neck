# Bill of Materials (Unitree)

Quantities are for one neck.

## Purchased parts

| Qty | Part | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | [Unitree J288 Brushless Digital Servo (metal)](https://shop.unitree.com/products/brushless-digital-servo?variant=50927641919721) | Yaw and pitch servos, 12–24 V. The S288 (plastic) fits the same parts and costs less, but it only takes up to 12 V, and the metal gears and case take impacts better. |
| 1 | [Unitree Single-Bus Serial-to-USB Module](https://shop.unitree.com/products/brushless-digital-servo?variant=51764999028969) | USB interface for the servos |
| 1 | [Luxonis OAK-1 W](https://shop.luxonis.com/products/oak-1-w) | Camera |
| 1 | [Locking USB-C cable](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DCNFS6F2) | Camera to your computer |
| 1 | USB cable | Serial-to-USB module to your computer |
| 1 | Plug for the G1 24 V port | Servo power |
| 2 | Servo cables (included with the servos) | Module to yaw servo, yaw servo to pitch servo |
| ~1 m | 16–18 AWG silicone wire | Power runs |

The J288s run directly on the G1's 24 V port, so this version needs no converter. The S288 only takes up to 12 V, so plug it into the G1's 12 V port instead.
